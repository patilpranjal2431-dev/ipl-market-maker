❓ Help & Tutorial

⚠️ Virtual coins only. No real money.

How to play (5 steps)

  1. Choose a fixture and press Next Ball to simulate the chase.
  2. Watch the fair probability — the model's estimate that the chasing team wins.
  3. The market maker quotes a BID (sell to) and ASK (buy from), always around the fair value.
  4. Buy if you think the true probability is higher than the ask; sell if lower.
  5. Positions settle at 100 (win) or 0 (loss) at the end of the match.

Glossary

Win probability — model estimate that the chasing team wins.
Bid — price at which you can sell a contract.
Ask — price at which you can buy a contract.
Spread — ask minus bid; the market maker's edge.
Market maker — bot quoting both sides to earn the spread.
Inventory — net contracts the market maker holds.
Mark to market — revaluing positions at current fair price.
P&L — profit and loss.
Adverse selection — losses to informed traders.
Calibration — predicted probabilities match observed frequencies.
Brier score — mean squared error of probability forecasts.
Required run rate — runs per over needed to win.
Wickets in hand — remaining wickets (10 − wickets lost).

FAQ

Is this real money? No, virtual coins only.

Why can't I buy at fair price? The market maker earns the spread.

Why does the spread widen after a wicket? Uncertainty and event bonus increase, so the bot quotes wider to protect itself.

How is my P&L calculated? Cash changes on trades; open positions are marked to fair value; at settlement contracts pay 100 or 0.
